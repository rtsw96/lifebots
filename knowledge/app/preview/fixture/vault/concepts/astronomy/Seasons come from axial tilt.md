# Seasons come from axial tilt

> [!review] Test fixture: one source only.

**Claim.** The seasons come from the tilt of Earth's axis, not its distance from the Sun.

## Mechanism
The half of Earth leaning towards the Sun gets more direct light and longer days. See [[Day length changes with the seasons]].
