# Rough reference values for estimating meals

Starting points for photo estimates. They are approximate typical servings (Health Promotion Board-style figures and standard food tables, rounded). Real plates vary by 20–30%; adjust for what the photo shows, especially rice or noodle portion, oil and skin.

The dish table leans Singaporean (hawker food). For other cuisines, start from standard food tables (the single foods below, or a national food database) and the same habits: judge the portion, add hidden oil.

## Servings and fibre

- **Vegetable serving:** ¾ cup cooked (about 75–80 g) or 1 cup raw leafy greens. A hawker "side of greens" is usually ~1 serving. Fibre about 2–3 g per serving.
- **Fruit serving:** 1 medium fruit (apple, pear, orange), 1 cup berries, 2 kiwis. Fibre about 3–5 g.
- **Hidden fat:** hawker stir-fries and fried items often carry 1–2 tbsp of oil (120–240 kcal) not visible in the photo. Estimate it in.
